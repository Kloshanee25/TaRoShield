Tamil & Romanized Tamil Smishing Detection System

TaRoShield is a web-based system that detects SMS smishing attacks written in Tamil and Romanized Tamil. It helps users identify whether a received message is safe or malicious by analyzing the message content using machine learning. This system is designed to protect people from online fraud, fake job offers, prize scams, and banking threats.
