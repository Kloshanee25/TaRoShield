About TaRoShield

TaRoShield is an intelligent SMS security platform designed to detect and prevent smishing (SMS phishing) attacks written in Tamil and Romanized Tamil. Smishing has become one of the most common cyber threats, where attackers use fake text messages to trick users into sharing personal, banking, or login information.

Unlike traditional spam filters, TaRoShield focuses specifically on messages used in Sri Lanka and Tamil-speaking communities. Many scams today are written in local languages or mixed Tamil-English formats, which makes them difficult to detect using standard security tools. TaRoShield is built to solve this problem by understanding the unique language patterns used in these messages.

Our system uses machine learning techniques to analyze the content of SMS messages. It examines words, sentence structures, suspicious links, urgency patterns, and known scam phrases to decide whether a message is safe or malicious. This allows users to quickly check any SMS before clicking links or replying.

TaRoShield helps protect people from:

  • Fake banking alerts
  • Prize and lottery scams
  • Job offer frauds
  • Account verification scams
  • Malicious links and phishing attempts

Our goal is to provide a simple, fast, and reliable tool that anyone can use to stay safe from SMS-based cyber threats. By supporting both Tamil and Romanized Tamil, TaRoShield makes cybersecurity accessible to a wider community and helps reduce digital fraud.
